# Privacy Policy

## Information CU Ways stores

CU Ways stores account names, emails, password hashes, and optional phone numbers and LINE IDs. It stores professional profiles and service listings, survey metadata and links, job and offer records, brief and proof links, payment confirmations, and reviews. CU Ways keeps survey questions and answers with the external survey provider.

## Who can see information

Signed-in Creators can view Marketer profiles, services, and verified reviews. Job participants can view their job records. Contact details are available to their owner, administrators, and the other participant after hiring. The job pages stop showing contact details when a job is cancelled. Reviews can display the reviewer's name and job reference on the Marketer's profile.

## External links and payments

Survey forms, briefs, and delivery evidence may open on external services. Those services have their own privacy rules. Check link permissions before sharing. CU Ways stores the simulated payment amount, method, and status. Do not enter payment account details or card numbers into CU Ways.

## Account and data requests

You can update contact details in settings and manage your survey records. Contact the CU Ways project team through the channel that provided your access for account deactivation or data questions. Deactivation ends access to the account. Job history can remain available to its other participant and administrators.
