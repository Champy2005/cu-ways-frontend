# Terms of Service

## Using CU Ways

CU Ways helps survey creators hire marketers to distribute surveys. Keep your account details accurate and protect your password. Use the platform only for surveys and outreach you are authorized to run. Respect the rules of the communities and services where you share surveys.

## Jobs and deliverables

Creators and marketers agree to the work and price through an accepted offer. Creators provide accessible survey and brief links. Marketers submit an accessible proof link for the Creator to review. Include only material you have permission to share. A Creator can cancel an unpaid job before completion.

## Payments

CU Ways records the Creator's payment confirmation. Participants arrange payment outside the platform. A Paid or Released status records a step in the job workflow. CU Ways does not collect, hold, or transfer funds.

## Reviews and accounts

Write reviews based on work you received through CU Ways. Self-hiring, fake reviews, harassment, and misuse of another person's contact details are prohibited. The project team can deactivate accounts that misuse the platform.

## Questions

Contact the CU Ways project team through the channel that provided your access. For data handling details, read the Privacy Policy.
