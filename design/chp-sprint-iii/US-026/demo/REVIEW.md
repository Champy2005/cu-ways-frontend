handoff in Light and Dark against the story requirements, shared theme, selected
Round 10 direction and current frontend references. The review covered all 14
story states, desktop and mobile views, visible pending states, narrow and
enlarged text, long links, focus, and motion/transparency fallbacks. Focused

suggestion to left-align the saved-only desktop card was declined: the centered
single-column layout was deliberately chosen and no accepted requirement
requires the alternative. Original evidence and source identity are retained
in the workspace at
`.artifacts/work/workforce-campaign-20261027/us026-review-adjudication.md`.
